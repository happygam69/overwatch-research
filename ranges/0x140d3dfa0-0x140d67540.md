### 2. The `v[…]` grammar and `@N/#N` RLE (T79-NEW: the particle-def text format)

`FUN_140d55940` (and the smaller `FUN_140d50d30` inside the string-table deserializer) parse a
**versioned, brace-delimited, run-length-compressed text format**:

- `v` `<int>` `[` … `]` — version 3 or 4 (`"Expected version between %d and %d (inclusive)"`);

- `'…'` single-quoted strings with `\\trfn^'ha` escapes (`FUN_140d5b120`).

definition records** the pass-52 manifest loader would carry. The `0x143116898..8c8` and
