## What this range is

**Engine runtime, not the registry machine** (evidence: `registrations: 0`, `records: 0`, no
`cd360`/`d4f30`/`d4f70`/`d4fb0`/`c2d50`/`c2d70`/`c7880`/`atexit` call anywhere in 336 files).
The range is a single stratum of gameplay/UI-runtime code that *consumes* the registry data
built in `0x14003ffe0`–`0x14054ea50`:

1. two "compose the app object" functions that allocate and attach ~150 subsystems (zone 1–2),
2. a container/allocator/hash-map library (~40 % of the files), used by everything after,
3. the **first plaintext settings reads** (the console-variable names registered in pass 26/27
   are read here through a family of accessors),
4. a chat/voice (Vivox) subsystem with named channels and a 0x288-B channel object,
5. a hotfix/integrity verifier with SHA-256 comparison and three anti-tamper string literals,
6. spatial queries (raycast/LOS) and entity-component access by 64-bit component id.

Nothing here registers a type; everything here looks types up.
