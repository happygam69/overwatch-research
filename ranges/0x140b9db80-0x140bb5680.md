# Pass 66 — chunks `0595`–`0602`, `.text` `0x140b9db80`–`0x140bb5680`

The range opens exactly where pass 65's boundary state said it would: the resolver
class of the world-side overhead-UI row engine, grown into one cascade. 0595, 0596
and 0598 are three more **full emissions of the five-variant row-field resolver**
(`FUN_140b9db80`, `FUN_140b9ee10`, `FUN_140ba6450`), and 0597 adds two smaller ones
(`FUN_140ba2cd0`, `FUN_140ba5160`) — five new members of the family pass 65 opened
with `FUN_140b92500/93c40/957a0/9a790/97370/9c720`. In 0595 the whole arm set is

semantics**: the scalar (or float4) leaves the resolver through a **0x50-B
ramp/lerp descriptor** and is written as a float4. 0597's `FUN_140ba2560` and
0599's `FUN_140ba8a10` are the first *real* (multi-caller) arm bodies, so the
sub-kind ladder now has meanings, not just dispatch sites.

Then the range turns: 0599–0600 is the **row-arm belt** that the kind table
dispatches into (row spawners, the sparse bitmap row grid, a 30-key curve
inverse solver, and the HTTP arm/result pair `FUN_140baaf50`/`FUN_140bab100`).
`FUN_140babb60` is the **first kind-record registrar** seen — a 0xb8 descriptor
with an explicit kind byte, memmoved into a static. And 0601–0602 are the

its protocol half at `0x140a7f…`; CODE_MAP ledger 76 called this band "the
`0x140ba/bb` voice-channel manager" — it is, and it is also the *tracker-kind
registry* that every voice operation rides on). The last entry
`FUN_140bb5680` (222-K/4821) closes the range inside the **Battle.net

`WAIT_REFERRAL`, `0x143870ED8–0x143870F70`).
